## 🕹️ About

Phantom Blade Zero is a dark wuxia action RPG set in a kungfupunk world of assassins, occult forces, machines, and folk horror. You play Soul, a condemned warrior with only 66 days to uncover the truth behind his master's death. This Windows build is an unofficial, raw development snapshot that surfaced ahead of the official release.

For players who want brutal action combat, stylish wuxia violence, strange machinery, and an unpolished underground build atmosphere.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- ⚔️ **Kung Fu Combat** — Fast, cinematic melee combat built around authentic kung fu techniques.
- 🩸 **Dark Wuxia World** — Explore a dangerous martial world filled with conspiracies, monsters, and rival masters.
- 🗡️ **Massive Arsenal** — Unlock more than 30 main weapons and 25 secondary Phantom Edges.
- 🔧 **Build Customization** — Upgrade weapons and combine accessories to shape your combat style.
- 🗺️ **Connected Exploration** — Move through interconnected regions, hidden paths, secrets, and side quests.
- ☠️ **Three Difficulty Modes** — Choose between Wayfarer, Hellwalker, and the brutal Sixty-Six Days mode.
- ⏳ **A Failing Clock** — Soul has 66 days to expose the consalternative before his temporary cure expires.
